Money in flight
—

Captured funds held in the clearing account, between leaving the payer and reaching the payee. Every capture adds exactly its amount and every settlement removes exactly its amount, so this returning to zero is proof that every capture that ever committed has settled.

Retries are free

Sends one payment twice with the same Idempotency-Key. The second request must return the identical body and charge nothing.

The journal behind that payment

Every entry's legs must sum to exactly zero. Debits left, credits right.

Break it on purpose

Arms the engine's failure-injection points at runtime. Send traffic with a point armed and watch requests fail — then disarm, and watch money in flight drain to zero with the books still balanced.
